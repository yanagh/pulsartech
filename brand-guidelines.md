# Pulsar Brand Guidelines

## Brand Position

Pulsar is a B2B technology company that helps businesses run better with AI, automation, business software, and smart camera systems.

The brand should feel:

- Simple
- Practical
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Business-focused
- Easy to understand

Pulsar should not feel overly technical, cold, complicated, or full of buzzwords.

## Core Message

Pulsar makes business easier to run.

Use simple language that explains clear business benefits:

- Save time
- Reduce mistakes
- Improve control
- See what is happening
- Automate repeated work
- Help teams work better
- Make better decisions faster

Avoid leading with technical explanations. Start with the business problem, then explain how Pulsar helps.

## Logo

Use the existing Pulsar logo as provided.

Do not edit, redraw, recolor, stretch, crop, or modify the logo unless a new approved logo file is provided.

Recommended usage:

- Place the logo on clean white, near-black, or simple dark backgrounds.
- Keep enough clear space around it.
- Do not place it on noisy backgrounds.
- Do not add effects, shadows, outlines, or filters to the logo.

### Visual Direction

Use dark sections with purple, green, and teal glow accents for a modern AI/automation feel.

Use bright blurs and soft glass effects carefully. They should make the page feel alive, not busy.

Avoid flat purple text on purple or dark purple backgrounds. Always check contrast.

## Typography

Use a clean system sans-serif stack:

```css
font-family: Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif;
```

Headlines should be bold and direct.

Georgian headlines should be slightly smaller than English headlines because Georgian letterforms look visually heavier at large sizes.

Recommended style:

- Hero headline: bold, clear, not oversized
- Section headlines: short and practical
- Body copy: simple, readable, business-friendly
- Buttons: short action phrases

## Voice and Tone

The writing should be simple enough for a business owner who does not know technology.

Use:

- Short sentences
- Clear benefits
- Everyday business language
- Confident but friendly tone

Avoid:

- Heavy technical language
- Long explanations
- Generic AI buzzwords
- Over-promising
- Complicated product names

Good examples:

- "We make your business easier to run."
- "Save time every day."
- "See what is happening in one place."
- "Automate repeated work."

Weak examples:

- "Leverage cutting-edge AI-driven digital transformation."
- "Enterprise-grade intelligent operational orchestration."
- "Unlock scalable synergies across your workflow ecosystem."

## Layout Style

The Pulsar website should feel modern, calm, and useful.

Recommended layout principles:

- Use strong spacing.
- Keep sections focused.
- Alternate dark and light sections.
- Use glass-style cards where useful.
- Keep card corners modest, around `8px`.
- Avoid cluttered dashboards in the hero.
- Do not over-explain inside visual cards.
- Use simple proof points instead of complex UI mockups.

## Hero Section

The hero should be clear, modern, and sales-focused.

Recommended structure:

- Small high-contrast eyebrow pill
- Strong headline
- Simple supporting copy
- Primary call-to-action
- Secondary call-to-action
- 2-3 short benefit points

The hero should not include a heavy dashboard, complicated panel, or too much text on the right side.

## Language System

Georgian is the primary language.

English is secondary and should live on a separate SEO-friendly page.

Recommended structure:

- Georgian: `/`
- English: `/en/`

Use language metadata:

- `html lang="ka"` for Georgian
- `html lang="en"` for English
- `hreflang="ka"`
- `hreflang="en"`
- `hreflang="x-default"`

Language switch should be a compact `KA / EN` pill.

## Buttons

- Purple background
- White text
- Clear action
- Short label

Examples:

- "დაგვიკავშირდით"
- "დარეკეთ Pulsar-ში"
- "Talk to us"
- "Call Pulsar"

Secondary buttons:

- Glass or outlined style on dark backgrounds
- White or near-black text depending on background
- Lower visual weight than the primary button

## Cards

Cards should be simple and readable.

Use cards for:

- Services
- Industries
- Benefits
- Steps

Avoid fake letter icons such as `A`, `B`, `C`, `R`, or `S` unless they are part of a real icon system.

If icons are used, they should be relevant and consistent.

Acceptable icon themes:

- Automation
- AI assistant
- Reports
- Business control
- Retail
- Construction
- Cameras
- Data
